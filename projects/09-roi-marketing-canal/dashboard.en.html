What the data shows

  • Where should we cut now? Google Ads and Meta Ads account for 65% of 12-month spend ($1,469,932 MXN) and sit below the 3:1 benchmark.
  • Which channels deserve more budget? Referrals (19.25:1), Email Marketing (13.01:1), and SEO / Organic (7.43:1) post the best ratios, yet receive only 19% of combined spend.
  • Can Referrals scale without limit? No — its CAC is up +14.6% and new customers are down -11.5% over 12 months: a sign of a capacity ceiling. Scale it cautiously, directing the bulk of the budget increase to the other efficient channels.
  • Does Google Ads deserve the same patience? No — CAC is down -3.1% and new customers are up +2.4% over 12 months: no clear improvement, and still below benchmark. The leading candidate for a budget cut.
  • Should we abandon Meta Ads? Not yet — its CAC is down -10.5% and new customers are up +28.2% over 12 months: it's an at-risk channel that is improving. Worth continuing to optimize before cutting.
  • How much money is at stake? $1,469,932 MXN over the last 12 months went to channels that don't cover the minimum expected return.
  • Is Events / Trade Shows, with its high spend, safe? Its ratio is healthy (5.74:1) but cooling (new customers -7.1% over 12 months) — maintain the budget and monitor before scaling it further.
  • Is the problem resolving on its own? No — the share of spend in at-risk channels held between 62.2% and 68.3% over the last 12 months, with no clear improving trend: it calls for an active reallocation decision, not waiting for it to fix itself.
