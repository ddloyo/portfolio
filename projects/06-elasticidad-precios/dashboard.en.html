What the data shows

  • Specialized Part X is the only product with enough evidence (R²=0.56, elasticity -0.6) to raise its price in the next list without needing a pilot test — estimated impact +$234,648 MXN/year.
  • 3 products (Specialized Part Y, Installation Service, Critical Spare Part) point toward a price increase (+$1,325,642 MXN/year combined) but with R² below 0.48 — before applying it across the full catalog, a pilot test in one region or customer segment is advisable.
  • 3 products (Component A, Standard Accessory, Component B) have solid evidence (R² 0.56–0.92) of being highly elastic — raising their price would destroy $2,173,463 MXN/year; they should not be touched this round.
  • Statistical confidence (R²) matters as much as the direction of elasticity: two products with the same estimated elasticity may warrant different actions if one has far more signal than noise than the other — that's why the matrix crosses both axes instead of ranking by elasticity alone.
