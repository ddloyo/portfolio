What the Data Shows

  • $1,264,998 MXN (78.5%) of outstanding receivables sits with just 2 clients — Importadora Tres Ríos and Servicios Integrales Roble, both in the Delinquent segment. Cash-flow risk isn't diversified: it's concentrated, and it should be managed with dedicated follow-up on those 2 clients, not a generic collections campaign.
  • The consolidated DSO (36.4 days) masks the fact that the Delinquent segment takes an average of 102 days to pay — almost 3 times longer. Reporting only the consolidated DSO understates the real risk; DSO by segment should be the number tracked month to month.
  • The 3 clients in the On-Time segment (Grupo Ferretero del Bajío, Distribuidora Maple, Grupo Industrial Norte) don't have a single peso outstanding today — 100% of receivables sit in the Slow-Pay and Delinquent segments. Collections effort should concentrate there, not be spread evenly across all 8 clients.
  • The aging-based collections curve projects that much of the Delinquent receivables will be collected in the next 4 weeks — but that curve is generic and doesn't use the actual history of these clients, who have taken 100+ days to pay in the past. For the Delinquent segment, that projection should be read as the optimistic scenario, not the expected one.
  • Monthly collections grew from $398,170 MXN in 2026-03 to $1,117,124 MXN in the latest complete month — a seemingly healthy trend, but the risk concentration in 2 clients hasn't changed along with it. Aggregate collections improving doesn't mean receivables risk has gone down.
  • Of the 5 clients with overdue invoices today, the 2 in the Delinquent segment account for 85% of the overdue amount and require the most urgent action (freeze new credit + payment plan), not just a collections call — see this week's checklist.
  • Credit policy recommendation: require an advance payment or reduce the credit limit for any client that falls into the Delinquent profile (more than 70 days average historical payment time) before they accumulate more overdue receivables — otherwise, risk concentration will keep growing with every new credit sale to those clients.
