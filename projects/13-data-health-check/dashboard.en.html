What the data shows

  • The database's overall health score is 73.1/100 (at risk): Invoices is the table dragging it down the most, with a score of 68.1.
  • The highest-impact issue is fecha in Invoices (fecha must be captured in standard ISO format (yyyy-mm-dd), not mixed with dd/mm/yyyy or mm/dd/yyyy.) -- 1024 rows (28.48%) fail the rule, with 11.39 points of score impact.
  • We detected 289 duplicate rows across the 4 tables and 282 broken references (cliente_id/sku values that no longer exist in their catalog) in Invoices -- both inflate any report that sums rows or joins tables without deduplicating/filtering first.
  • The hardest case to spot at a glance: 332 invoices (9.5%) where total doesn't match cantidad × precio_unitario -- a manual override without recalculation that no null or duplicate filter catches.
  • We identified 3 orphaned columns (100% empty, unused) in the schema -- they don't penalize the score because they aren't a defect in captured data, but they are pending schema cleanup.
