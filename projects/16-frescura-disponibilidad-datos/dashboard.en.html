What the data shows

  • Today 17 of 22 tables (6 of 10 sources) are within their SLA. In error: sucursales.pedido_sucursal_monterrey has gone 79.0 h without loading (error at 72 h); ads.marketing_gasto has gone 4.2 d without loading (error at 96 h). In warning: sucursales.pedido_sucursal_guadalajara (70.0 h), wms.inventario_snapshot (31.0 h), scorecard.kpi_captura_manual (38.0 d).
  • sucursales is the most frequently late source: outside SLA on 19 of 30 days (53.9% of checks in pass). The pattern is weekly: Sunday and Monday meet 3% of checks and the rest of the week 79%. That process does not load on weekends, so the 36 h warn threshold breaks every week with nothing actually wrong. A threshold that fires every week teaches people to ignore the alert: fix the process or the threshold.
  • ads is the other chronic case: 12 days outside SLA across 5 episodes, even with a threshold far looser than pos's (warn 48 h / error 96 h, versus 6 h / 12 h). An ads feed tolerates lag because of the attribution window, but not 4.2 d. It feeds the dashboard 09 Marketing ROI.
  • 5 of 10 sources meet ≥95% of checks (crm, producto_analytics, pos, encuestas, helpdesk); their failures are one-off incidents, not a pattern. The longest run outside SLA was 83 h (sucursales). Today's average staleness is 89% of the warn threshold: tables in pass sit at 65% of their margin and the failing ones are already at 170%.
  • How to read this dashboard: the thresholds and today's status are real (the freshness blocks in the warehouse's _*__sources.yml and dbt's sources.json). The 30-day history is simulated, but classified with the same rule: 154 comparisons against live dbt source freshness match (see data/verify_dbt_parity.py).
